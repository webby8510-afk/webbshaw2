Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is the demo really completely free?
Yes — completely. We build a real working website using your actual business name, details and content. No payment, no deposit, nothing to sign. You only pay once you've seen it and decided you want to go ahead.
How long does it take to go live?
Demo ready within 48 hours of submitting the form. Once you approve it and pay the deposit, your live site is usually up within 2–3 business days. From first contact to live website — under 7 days.
What does the £75 per month actually cover?
Everything. Hosting, domain renewal, SSL certificate, security updates, backups and the WhatsApp management service. Text us any change — new prices, photos, updated hours — and we handle it within 36 business hours. No tech knowledge ever required.
Do I need to provide photos?
It helps but it's not essential for the demo. We can use photos from your Instagram, Google Business Profile or anywhere publicly visible. If you have nothing we'll use appropriate placeholders and swap them for real photos when you have them.
Can I cancel the monthly retainer?
Yes — 30 days written notice and that's it. No penalties, no locked-in periods. If you cancel, all website files are provided within 14 days so you can host it elsewhere. Provided all invoices are settled.
Will my website actually show up on Google?
That's the whole point. Every site includes full local SEO — structured data markup, meta titles and descriptions targeting local searches, Google Business Profile integration and fast loading. Built to be found, not just to look good.
